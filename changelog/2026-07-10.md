> ([Better tools made Copilot code review worse. Here's how we actually improved it.](https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/better-tools-made-copilot-code-review-worse-heres-how-we-actually-improved-it/), セクション "The result: roughly 20% lower average review cost")
